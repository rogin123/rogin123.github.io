About Me

I'm currently a Master's in City Planning student at MIT concentrating in environmental policy. Before that, I spent four years as a data scientist at the Urban Institute where my research focused on the intersection of housing, climate adaptation, and disaster resilience. I graduated from Northwestern University 2020 (a famously fantastic time to be finishing college) where I studied environmental science, data science, and Middle Eastern studies.

When I'm not typing away at my computer for school, I like to type away at my computer futzing around with data projects, read about how the internet is influencing society, bike, hike, and be a gen Z about town in Cambridge.
